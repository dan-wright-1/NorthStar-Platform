# NorthStar Retail — Shipping Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SHP-007
**Effective:** 2026-03-01
**Owner:** Fulfillment Operations
**Applies to:** northstar.com orders shipped within the US, Canada, and Mexico

---

## 1. Shipping Options and Rates

| Method | Transit time | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $7.95 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 |
| Store pickup | 2–4 hours | Free |

Transit times are measured from **dispatch**, not from order placement. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET dispatch the following business day. Orders placed on a weekend or holiday dispatch the next business day.

Overnight orders must be placed before **12:00 PM ET** to dispatch the same day.

---

## 2. Free Shipping Thresholds

Free standard shipping applies at the following order subtotals, after discounts and before tax:

| Tier | Free standard shipping at |
|---|---|
| Non-member | $99 |
| Bronze | $75 |
| Silver | $50 |
| Gold | Any amount |
| Platinum | Any amount |

Free shipping applies to **standard** shipping only. Expedited and overnight are charged at full rate for all tiers, with no exception. Gold and Platinum members do not receive free expedited shipping, and this is the most frequent source of customer confusion on this policy.

Gift card purchases do not count toward the free shipping threshold.

---

## 3. Store Pickup

Store pickup is free at all 400 locations. Orders are typically ready within 2–4 hours during store hours and are held for **7 calendar days**, after which the order is cancelled and refunded automatically.

Not all merchandise is eligible for store pickup. Oversized items — kayaks, canoes, rooftop cargo boxes, and furniture over 50 lbs — ship directly and cannot be routed to a store.

---

## 4. Oversized and Hazardous Items

**Oversized items** carry a surcharge of **$49.95** in addition to standard shipping. The surcharge applies at every tier and is **not waived by free shipping benefits**. Items subject to the surcharge are flagged on the product page.

**Hazardous materials** — fuel canisters, bear spray, lithium batteries above 100Wh — ship ground only, cannot be expedited, and cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or Mexico. These restrictions are regulatory and cannot be waived by any employee or system.

---

## 5. International and Remote Destinations

### 5.1 Canada

Standard rates apply. Duties and taxes are collected at checkout under Delivered Duty Paid terms. Transit adds 2–3 business days beyond the stated window.

### 5.2 Mexico

Mexico orders ship from the Monterrey distribution center and carry a separate rate card. Free shipping thresholds **do not apply to Mexico orders** regardless of tier. Transit is 7–10 business days. Overnight service is not available.

### 5.3 Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories

Standard shipping only, 7–12 business days. Free shipping thresholds apply, but oversized surcharges are doubled.

---

## 6. Delivery Problems

### 6.1 Lost in Transit

A shipment with no carrier scan for **7 consecutive business days** is treated as lost. NorthStar will reship at no cost or refund in full, at the customer's choice. No claim form is required.

### 6.2 Damaged on Arrival

Report damage within **14 days** of delivery with photographs of the item and the outer packaging. NorthStar ships a replacement immediately and issues a prepaid return label for the damaged item. Original shipping is refunded.

### 6.3 Marked Delivered but Not Received

Customer service will open a carrier trace, which takes **3–5 business days**. If the trace is unsuccessful, NorthStar reships or refunds.

For orders above **$500**, a signed affidavit of non-receipt is required before reshipment. Customer service agents and automated systems **may not waive the affidavit requirement**; it exists for fraud control.

---

## 7. Address Changes and Cancellation

Orders may be modified or cancelled only before dispatch. Once an order has dispatched, it cannot be recalled, redirected, or cancelled, and the customer must use the returns process.

Because most orders dispatch within hours, the practical window for a change is short. Agents should set this expectation explicitly rather than promising an attempt that will usually fail.

---

## 8. Peak Season

Between **November 15 and December 26**, stated transit times are extended by 1–2 business days across all methods. Overnight service is suspended entirely from **December 20 through December 26**.

Free shipping thresholds are unchanged during peak season.
