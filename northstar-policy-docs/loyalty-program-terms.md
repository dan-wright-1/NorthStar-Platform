# NorthStar Summit Rewards — Program Terms

**Document ID:** POL-LOY-011
**Effective:** 2026-02-01
**Owner:** Loyalty & CRM
**Membership:** ~1.4M active members

---

## 1. Membership

Summit Rewards is free to join. Membership requires a valid email address and is limited to one account per person. Accounts may not be shared, combined, or transferred, including between household members.

---

## 2. Tiers

Tier is determined by **trailing 12-month spend**, evaluated on the first day of each month.

| Tier | Trailing 12-month spend | Members (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Under $500 | 61% |
| **Silver** | $500 – $1,999.99 | 26% |
| **Gold** | $2,000 – $4,999.99 | 10% |
| **Platinum** | $5,000 and above | 3% |

Spend counts merchandise only. Gift card purchases, taxes, shipping charges, and amounts refunded through returns do **not** count toward tier qualification. A return reduces trailing spend and can cause a tier downgrade.

### 2.1 Tier Upgrades

Upgrades take effect **immediately** upon crossing a threshold. A customer whose qualifying purchase pushes trailing spend to $2,000 becomes Gold at the moment the transaction settles and may use Gold benefits on that same visit.

### 2.2 Tier Downgrades

Downgrades are evaluated monthly and take effect on the first of the month. A member whose trailing 12-month spend falls below their tier threshold is moved down **one tier per evaluation**, never more than one level per month.

Members receive email notice **30 days before** a scheduled downgrade.

### 2.3 Tier Protection

A member who has held Gold or Platinum for **24 consecutive months or longer** is protected from a single downgrade evaluation once per rolling 24-month period. Protection applies automatically and cannot be requested, deferred, or transferred.

---

## 3. Points

Members earn **1 point per $1** of qualifying merchandise spend.

| Tier | Earn rate | Effective value |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 1× | 1 point per $1 |
| Silver | 1.25× | 1.25 points per $1 |
| Gold | 1.5× | 1.5 points per $1 |
| Platinum | 2× | 2 points per $1 |

**100 points = $5** in redemption value. Points may be redeemed in increments of 100, in store or online, and may be combined with promotions unless a promotion states otherwise.

### 3.1 Points Expiration

Points expire **18 months after the member's most recent qualifying purchase**, not 18 months after the points were earned. Any qualifying purchase resets the expiration clock on the member's entire balance.

Members receive expiration warnings at 60 days and 14 days before expiry.

**This provision matters for retention.** A lapsed member with a large balance has a concrete, quantifiable reason to return, and that balance is the single most effective lever in a win-back offer.

### 3.2 Points and Returns

Points earned on a returned item are deducted when the refund is processed. If the deduction would take the balance below zero, the balance is set to zero and no debt is carried.

Points already redeemed against a subsequent purchase are not clawed back.

## 4. Tier Benefits

| Benefit | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Points earn rate | 1× | 1.25× | 1.5× | 2× |
| Birthday reward | $5 | $10 | $20 | $40 |
| Free standard shipping | $75+ | $50+ | Always | Always |
| Free return shipping | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Return window | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days | **60 days** |
| Early access to sales | No | 12 hours | 24 hours | 48 hours |
| Free gear repair | No | No | 2 per year | Unlimited |
| Dedicated support line | No | No | No | Yes |
| Annual gear credit | No | No | No | $100 |

Benefits are tied to the member's tier at the time the benefit is used, except the return window, which is fixed at the tier held **at the time of purchase** (see POL-RET-004 §1.1).

### 4.1 Annual Gear Credit

Platinum members receive a **$100 gear credit** each January 1. The credit expires December 31 of the same year, does not carry over, has no cash value, and is forfeited if the member is downgraded below Platinum before using it.

---

## 5. Promotional Offers

Targeted offers issued to a member are **single-use, non-transferable, and tied to the member account**. Offers may not be stacked with other targeted offers, though a targeted offer may generally be combined with points redemption.

Offer codes are void if the account is closed, if the offer's stated conditions are not met, or after the stated expiry date. Expired offers **cannot be reinstated by customer service or by any automated system.**

### 5.1 Retention Offers

Retention offers are generated for members identified as at risk of lapsing. These offers:

- Are valid for **45 days** from issue unless stated otherwise
- Are limited to **one active retention offer per member** at a time
- May not be combined with another retention offer
- Do not extend, alter, or waive any provision of the returns policy

An offer that promises a benefit inconsistent with these terms — a return window longer than the member's tier allows, a discount stacked beyond the stated limit, or a benefit belonging to a higher tier — is **invalid**, and honoring it requires Director-level approval.

---

## 6. Account Closure

A member may close their account at any time. Closure forfeits all points immediately, and points are not reinstated if the member rejoins.

NorthStar may close an account for abuse of the program, including returns abuse under POL-RET-004 §8, fraudulent points activity, or reselling of member-exclusive merchandise.

Accounts with **no qualifying purchase for 36 consecutive months** are closed automatically, and all points are forfeited at closure.

---

## 7. Changes to the Program

NorthStar may modify these terms with **60 days' notice** to active members by email. Continued use of the program after the effective date constitutes acceptance. Changes that reduce the value of already-earned points require **90 days' notice**.
