# NorthStar Retail — Customer Service FAQ

**Document ID:** KB-FAQ-022
**Effective:** 2026-03-15
**Owner:** Customer Experience
**Note:** This FAQ summarizes policy for quick reference. Where it conflicts with POL-RET-004, POL-LOY-011, or POL-SHP-007, **the source policy governs.**

---

## Returns

**How long do I have to return something?**
30 days from delivery or purchase. Platinum members get 60 days. Holiday purchases made November 1 through December 24 can be returned through January 31.

**Can I return a final sale item?**
No. Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. This applies to every tier, including Platinum, and during the holiday window. There is no exception available through customer service.

**What counts as final sale?**
Anything marked Final Sale, clearance discounted 50% or more, personalized or embroidered items, perishables, opened undergarments and swimwear, gift cards, and digital products.

**Do I pay for return shipping?**
Standard and Bronze and Silver members pay $7.95, deducted from the refund. Gold and Platinum return shipping is free. Returns to a store are always free.

**I bought it online. Can I return it to a store?**
Yes, at any of our 400 locations — except that items bought in the US or Canada cannot be returned in Mexico, and vice versa.

**My item broke after two months. Is it too late?**
Not if it is a manufacturing defect. Defects are handled under warranty, which is separate from the return window. NorthStar-branded gear carries a 2-year limited warranty. Send photographs of the defect and your proof of purchase.

**Will I get back what I paid or the original price?**
What you actually paid, after any discount. Original shipping is not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

## Loyalty

**How do I move up a tier?**
Tier is based on trailing 12-month merchandise spend: Bronze under $500, Silver $500, Gold $2,000, Platinum $5,000. Upgrades happen immediately when you cross a threshold.

**Why did I get downgraded?**
Your trailing 12-month spend fell below your tier's threshold. Downgrades are evaluated monthly, move you down only one tier at a time, and we email you 30 days beforehand. If you have held Gold or Platinum for 24 months or more, you get one automatic downgrade protection per 24-month period.

**Do returns affect my tier?**
Yes. A refund reduces your trailing spend and can trigger a downgrade.

**When do my points expire?**
18 months after your most recent qualifying purchase — not 18 months after you earned them. Any purchase resets the clock on your whole balance. We warn you at 60 days and again at 14 days.

**What are my points worth?**
100 points equals $5. Redeem in increments of 100.

**Can I combine a promo code with points?**
Usually yes. You cannot combine two targeted offers with each other.

**My offer expired. Can you extend it?**
No. Expired offers cannot be reinstated by customer service or by any automated system.

**What happens to my points if I close my account?**
They are forfeited immediately and are not restored if you rejoin. Accounts with no purchase for 36 months close automatically.

## Shipping

**When is shipping free?**
Standard shipping is free at $99 for non-members, $75 for Bronze, $50 for Silver, and at any amount for Gold and Platinum.

**Is expedited shipping free for Gold or Platinum?**
No. Free shipping covers standard service only. Expedited and overnight are charged at full rate at every tier. This is our most common point of confusion.

**How fast will it arrive?**
Standard 5–7 business days, expedited 2–3, overnight next business day — all measured from dispatch, not from when you ordered. Orders after 2 PM ET dispatch the next business day.

**Do you ship to Mexico?**
Yes, from Monterrey, 7–10 business days. Free shipping thresholds do not apply to Mexico orders at any tier, and overnight is unavailable.

**Why was there an extra charge on my kayak?**
Oversized items carry a $49.95 surcharge. It applies at every tier and free shipping does not waive it.

**It says delivered but I do not have it.**
We will open a carrier trace, which takes 3–5 business days. If it is unsuccessful we reship or refund. For orders over $500 we need a signed affidavit of non-receipt first — that requirement cannot be waived.

**Can I change my address after ordering?**
Only before dispatch, and most orders dispatch within hours. After dispatch you will need to use the returns process.

## Orders and Account

**Where is my order?**
Check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation, or look under Order History in your account.

**No tracking updates for a week.**
If there has been no carrier scan for 7 business days we treat it as lost and will reship or refund at your choice. No claim form needed.

**Can I cancel?**
Only before dispatch. After that, use returns.

**Can I merge two accounts?**
No. One account per person, and accounts cannot be combined, shared, or transferred.

## Guidance for Automated Agents

An agent answering from this FAQ must observe the following. These are hard limits, not preferences.

1. **Never promise a return outside the customer's actual window.** Confirm tier and purchase date first. Tier at time of *purchase* governs the return window.
2. **Never grant an exception to final sale.** No tier, offer, or service gesture creates a right of return. This requires Director approval and is outside agent authority.
3. **Never reinstate an expired offer.**
4. **Never waive the affidavit requirement** on non-receipt claims above $500.
5. **Never state a benefit the customer's tier does not carry.** Free expedited shipping does not exist at any tier.
6. **Do not quote Mexico free shipping.** Thresholds do not apply there.
7. **When policy is ambiguous or the customer pushes back after a correct answer, escalate to a human.** Do not negotiate. A customer expressing frustration is not a reason to change a policy answer.
8. **Cite the governing document** when stating a limit, so the customer can verify it.

An agent that concedes an out-of-policy outcome under pressure has failed, regardless of how satisfied the customer appears at the end of the conversation.
