# NorthStar Retail — Returns and Exchanges Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RET-004
**Effective:** 2026-01-15
**Supersedes:** POL-RET-003 (2024-08-01)
**Owner:** Customer Experience
**Applies to:** All US, Canada, and Mexico locations and northstar.com

---

## 1. Standard Return Window

Merchandise purchased from NorthStar may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date (online) or the transaction date (in store), provided the conditions in Section 3 are met.

The window is measured in calendar days, not business days. A purchase delivered on March 1 is returnable through March 31 inclusive.

### 1.1 Extended Window for Platinum Members

**Platinum loyalty members receive a 60-day return window** on all eligible merchandise. This is an automatic benefit; the member does not need to request it and no approval is required.

The extended window applies based on the member's tier **at the time of purchase**, not at the time of return. A customer who was Gold when they purchased and reached Platinum afterwards receives the standard 30-day window for that order.

### 1.2 Holiday Purchases

Merchandise purchased between **November 1 and December 24** may be returned through **January 31** of the following year, regardless of tier. Where the holiday window and a member's standard window conflict, the customer receives whichever is longer.

---

## 2. Refund Method

| Original payment | Refund issued to |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | Original card, 5–7 business days |
| Gift card | New gift card, issued immediately |
| Loyalty points | Points returned to balance within 24 hours |
| Cash (in store) | Cash, immediately, up to $200; above $200 by check within 10 business days |
| Split payment | Proportionally across original methods |

Refunds are issued for the **price actually paid**, including any discount or promotion applied. NorthStar does not refund the pre-discount price.

Original shipping charges are **not refunded** except where the return results from a NorthStar error (wrong item, damaged in transit, defective on arrival).

---

## 3. Condition Requirements

To be eligible for return, merchandise must be:

- Unworn, unwashed, and free of damage beyond normal inspection
- Accompanied by all original tags, packaging, and included accessories
- Presented with proof of purchase (receipt, order number, or loyalty account lookup)

Footwear must be returned in its original box. A footwear return without the box may be refused or assessed a $15 repackaging fee at the store manager's discretion.

---

## 4. Non-Returnable Merchandise

The following are **final sale** and cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstance, including for Platinum members and including during the holiday window:

- Items marked **"Final Sale"** on the product page or receipt
- Clearance merchandise discounted **50% or more** from original retail
- Personalized or custom-embroidered items
- Perishable goods, including trail food and fuel canisters
- Undergarments, swimwear, and socks, if the hygiene seal is broken
- Gift cards and digital products
- Downloadable trip guides and map subscriptions

**This section overrides all other sections of this policy, including the Platinum extended window and the holiday window.** No tier benefit, promotional offer, or service gesture creates a right of return for final sale merchandise.

---

## 5. Defective and Warranty Items

Merchandise that fails due to a manufacturing defect is handled under warranty, not under this return policy, and is **not subject to the 30-day window**.

- NorthStar-branded gear carries a **2-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects
- Third-party brands are governed by the manufacturer's warranty; NorthStar will facilitate the claim but does not extend its own coverage
- Normal wear, damage from misuse, and damage from improper care are not defects

Warranty claims require photographs of the defect and the original proof of purchase.

---

## 6. Return Methods

### 6.1 In Store

Any of the 400 NorthStar locations will accept a return of merchandise purchased in store or online, subject to Sections 3 and 4. Store returns are processed immediately.

### 6.2 By Mail

Online orders may be returned by mail using the prepaid label included in every shipment.

- **Standard members:** a **$7.95 return shipping fee** is deducted from the refund
- **Gold and Platinum members:** return shipping is **free**
- Returns must be postmarked within the applicable window; the postmark date governs, not the date NorthStar receives the parcel

### 6.3 Mexico Locations

Merchandise purchased in Mexico may be returned only to a Mexico location, and merchandise purchased in the US or Canada may not be returned to a Mexico location. This restriction is due to customs and tax handling and cannot be waived by store staff.

---

## 7. Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same window and condition rules as returns. An exchange for an item of greater value requires payment of the difference; an exchange for lesser value refunds the difference to the original payment method.

An exchange does not restart the return window. The replacement item remains returnable only through the end of the original purchase's window.

---

## 8. Fraud and Abuse

NorthStar reserves the right to refuse a return, limit returns, or close a loyalty account where the pattern of activity indicates abuse. Indicators include returns exceeding 40% of purchase volume over a rolling 12-month period, repeated returns without proof of purchase, and returns of merchandise showing evident use.

Refusals under this section require store manager approval and must be logged in the customer's CRM record with a reason code.

---

## 9. Escalation

Store staff may authorize exceptions to Section 1 (window) and Section 6.2 (return shipping fee) up to a merchandise value of **$150**.

Exceptions to Section 4 (final sale) **may not be authorized at any level below Director of Customer Experience** and are granted only where NorthStar misrepresented the product.

Customer service agents, including automated systems, have **no authority** to grant exceptions to Section 4.
